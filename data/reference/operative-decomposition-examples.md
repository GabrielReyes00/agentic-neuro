# Operative Decomposition Calibration Examples

Reference data only. Executable decomposition policy lives in
`.agents/shared/commands/intraoperative-guide-decomposition.md`.

## ACDF

Surface anterior-versus-posterior selection; subaxial biomechanics and fusion
rationale; laryngoscopy/RLN risk; side-of-approach physiology; longus colli and
sympathetic-chain mechanics; uncinate/vertebral-artery danger; PLL/cord/root
decompression endpoints; endplate preparation and graft mechanics; sagittal
alignment; arthroplasty/corpectomy/posterior alternatives; dysphagia, hematoma,
esophageal injury, pseudarthrosis, and airway surveillance. Address airway,
paralytic/MEP compatibility, cuff pressure, monitoring when indicated,
thyroidal/prevertebral bleeding, retractor-release inspection, decompression,
and hardware-position endpoints.

## Far Lateral Or Transcondylar Approach

Surface V3/V4/PICA/lower-cranial-nerve anatomy; the suboccipital triangle;
condyle, hypoglossal canal, and jugular tubercle drilling; instability from bone
removal; approach variants; vertebral-artery control; venous-plexus bleeding;
lower-cranial-nerve morbidity; CSF leak; relevant CN/SSEP/MEP monitoring;
position-related physiology; fusion thresholds; and alternative approaches.

## EVD Placement

Surface indication (hydrocephalus, ICP monitoring, or CSF diversion),
side/trajectory, coagulation, sterile setup, catheter landmarks, no-CSF
troubleshooting, drainage-system management, infection/hemorrhage/obstruction,
early surveillance, transition to permanent diversion, bedside sedation, and a
compact time-out/sterile-field communication plan.
